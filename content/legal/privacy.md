## Information we collect

### Information you give us

- **Free SEO checker.** To unlock the full report, you enter your name, email address, phone number and the website URL you want checked. We also store the score the checker produced.
- **Booking a call.** If you book a consultation through the scheduling widget on the Site, you give your details (for example name, email, phone and preferred time) to our scheduling provider, which shares them with us.
- **Email.** If you write to hello@riseridge.io, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message.

### Information collected automatically

- **When you submit the SEO checker form,** we also record technical details about your visit: IP address, approximate location derived from it (city, region, country), device type, browser, operating system, user-agent string, language, time zone, screen size and the referring page.
- **Microsoft Clarity.** We use Microsoft Clarity to understand how visitors use the Site, through behavioral metrics, heatmaps and session replay. Clarity collects usage data, such as pages viewed, clicks, scrolling and mouse movement, using first- and third-party cookies and similar technologies. For more on how Microsoft collects and uses data, see the [Microsoft Privacy Statement](https://www.microsoft.com/privacy/privacystatement).
- **Hosting and delivery.** Our hosting provider, Vercel, processes standard server and request logs (such as IP address, browser and requested page) to deliver and secure the Site. The Site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the fonts are requested.

We do not use advertising pixels or sell personal information.

## Cookies and your choices

You can also manage Microsoft's use of data at [choice.microsoft.com](https://choice.microsoft.com).
